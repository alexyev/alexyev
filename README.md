## 📌 What I'm working on

### fundme · `TypeScript` · `Postgres` · `Vercel`
Fundraising platform I am building for **Engineering at Carolina**, the student engineering org where I serve as VP for Student Development. TypeScript frontend, **Postgres** for data, deployed on **Vercel**.

### Systematic Equity Backtesting Platform · `Python`
Point-in-time-correct research and backtesting engine over a 25-year, 3000+ equity universe. Columnar storage in **Parquet**, vectorized signal computation with **pandas** / **NumPy**, portfolio construction with realistic transaction costs, and an LLM layer for hypothesis generation.

## 📦 Previously

### Cultura · `Next.js` · `Python` · `Supabase` · *2023–2025*
Crop phenology app for small-scale farmers. Weather ingestion via the **OpenWeatherMap API**, **Postgres** on Supabase, deployed on **Vercel**. Backed by Emergent Ventures, The Iris Project, and Ingenious+; presented at WebSummit Lisbon and SXSW.

### Carbon Credit Analytics · `pandas` · `NumPy`
Built at **Boomitra** (Earthshot Prize winner) to audit credit issuance across LATAM. Identified climate covariates driving soil carbon sequestration rates; results fed back into internal model weights.

### Rehabilitation Glove + Unity Game · `C#` · `Arduino` · `MATLAB`
Sensor glove and interactive game for injury rehab at UNC's Experimental Engineering Lab. **81% pose accuracy** on the first prototype, with signal processing in MATLAB and assets built in Blender.
